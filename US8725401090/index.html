The model · how the treasure gets to the rack

A buyer, a vendor, a sticker.

TJX buys closeouts, cancellations, and overruns from 21,000 vendors the way an estate-sale shopper does — quickly, quietly, and at a price the next guy can't get. Then it prints a sticker.

The takeaway · receipt at the bottom

5,200 stores. One sticker, repeated.

Every day at TJX, the buyers find another deal somebody else couldn't move. The sticker goes on. The shopper finds it. Out of every dollar that comes back through the register, nine cents survive — and almost all of that goes back to the people who own the company. That's the recap.
