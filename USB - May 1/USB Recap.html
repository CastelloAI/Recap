The bet · capital out the door

Three checks,

What does a bank do with surplus cash? In 2025, U.S. Bancorp answered three ways at once.

Dividends $3.17B

Returned to shareholders. The base case — habitual, contractual, expected.

A small number with a big derivative. Buybacks nearly tripled — the surest signal that integration costs are clearing.

FY '25 · the takeaway

One hundred and sixty-two years. Still compounding.
