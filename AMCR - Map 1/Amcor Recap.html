09The takeaway

A dollar arrives.
Seven cents
survive.

Of every dollar in the door — resin, can, film, label — six and a half cents leave as operating profit. That's the packaging trade. Volume is the only way to make it big.

Operating margin · 7¢ Spent on the bottle · 93¢

Amcor opened FY'25 by absorbing a peer. It closes the year with a balance sheet to digest, a payout to defend, and $650M in promised synergies to make the math work. The plumbing keeps running while it does.
