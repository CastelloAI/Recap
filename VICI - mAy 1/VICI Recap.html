02The Mechanism · Triple-Net

Tenants pay everything.

Property taxes. Insurance. Maintenance. Capex. Under triple-net, the tenant carries it all — VICI just collects the check, indexed to CPI, locked for decades.

The cost of running the properties is less than one penny on the dollar — because VICI doesn't run them. Caesars and MGM do.

03Two Tenants · 74% of Rent

Two pillars hold
the whole house up.

Caesars Entertainment writes 39% of the annualized rent check. MGM Resorts writes another 35%. The remaining 91 properties supply the rest.
