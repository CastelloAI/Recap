The thesis opened with twenty-point-eight million packages a day. The page closes on what survives the journey: almost all of it goes back out the door — as wages, as fuel, as dividends. UPS is, in the end, a machine for converting parcels into payouts.

And now — quietly, deliberately — it is changing what's in the parcels.
