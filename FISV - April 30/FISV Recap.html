The takeaway

Every swipe pays a toll.
~18 cents survive.

Of every dollar that crosses the rails: forty-one is the cost of running them, twenty-one goes to selling and amortizing what was already bought, thirteen funds the next bet, and what's left is the residual. Then management hands ~130% of free cash flow back through the ticker, takes on more debt, and acquires the next decade of growth. The rails keep humming.
