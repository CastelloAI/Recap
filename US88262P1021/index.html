— Section II / Origin

A railroad failed. The land remained. A century later, the Permian Basin would sit on top of it — and TPL would never have to lift a drill.

The model · Two faucets

Other companies pull oil out of the ground.
TPL just owns the ground.

Two segments collect on every barrel produced: a royalty on the oil and gas, and a separate stream from selling water — both sourced from the same dirt, neither requiring TPL to drill a single well.

— Section V / The Bet

With no debt and a half-billion of dry powder, TPL is doing something unusual for a royalty business: it is buying more land — and beginning to lease it for things other than oil.

The bet · Capital deployed

Where the cash is going.

In 2025, TPL deployed capital in three directions — more dirt, more dividends, and a first bet on data centers — while leaving the credit line untouched.
