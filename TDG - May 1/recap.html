TDG TransDigm Recap · FY '25
01Thesis

Almost every aircraft flying today carries a part they own.

TransDigm Group designs, produces and supplies highly engineered aerospace components for nearly all commercial and military aircraft in service. Roughly 90% proprietary. The cockpit doesn't know it. The income statement does.

NYSE · TDG Cleveland, OH Founded 1993

Sixteen-thousand five-hundred people. Fifty-five units. One thesis: own the part nobody else can stamp.

02How the money flies in

The plane is sold once.
The parts are sold forever.

Roughly three out of every five dollars come from the aftermarket — replacement parts and repair services for aircraft already in service. The rest comes from OEMs putting new airframes together. The aftermarket is where the margin lives.

06The bet · the balance sheet

They don't run the company for the company.

TransDigm runs a private-equity capital structure in the open. Borrow against the cash flows. Push the proceeds out the door. Buy the next part-maker. Repeat.

09The takeaway

A part on every plane.
A third of every dollar stays.

We opened with a thesis: a part on nearly every plane. We close with the receipt: nearly a third of every dollar that lifts off survives the runway. Then it goes back out the door — to debt, to dividends, to the next bolt-on. And the loop begins again.
