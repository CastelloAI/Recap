02 · How it makes money

One trailer. Many shippers. Priced by the pound.

Charged by weight, distance, and class. Fuel surcharges ride along. Every shipment shares the trailer with twenty others bound the same direction.

04 · Where the money goes

The trailer, billed by line item.

ODFL's cost structure is labor-heavy by design. Their drivers, dockworkers, and dispatchers move every freight bill — and take roughly half of every dollar that comes through the door.

What rolls out the back doors: 24.8¢ on every dollar.
