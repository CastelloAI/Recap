ARE Alexandria Real Estate
Recap · FY '25
The thesis · NYSE: ARE

35.9M sq ft of American science, leased by the decade.

Alexandria doesn't make the drugs. It owns the buildings the drugs are made in — 340 properties clustered in eight zip codes where American biotech actually happens.

Behind every lab,
a landlord. Behind every landlord, a balance sheet.

●   The business · how the rent flows

A triple-net machine for science.

Pharma and biotech tenants sign long, hard-to-break leases on Class A/A+ lab space. Rent and operating costs both pass to the tenant. 75% of annual rental revenue comes from the same Megacampus properties — clusters where labs cluster on purpose.

A side hustle on top: a venture-capital arm that writes checks to the same biotech firms that lease the labs — booking gains when those bets exit.
