07 · The bet · capital

An asset-light ledger, returning to itself.

Capex is a rounding error — $43M against $2.45B of operating cash. The plan: route roughly half of free cash flow back to shareholders through buybacks in FY27, and tuck-in acquisitions for cloud, construction, and AI.

09 · The takeaway

Of every dollar Autodesk bills, 91¢ make it past the cost of revenue

The hero opened with 100 million users drawing the world. The page closes here, on the math: a 91% gross margin, a 21.9% operating margin, and a $2.41B river of free cash that bends back into the firm.

Forty-four years after John Walker shipped a drafting program for the PC, the line is still drawing — only now it draws itself, every quarter, into the next subscription.
