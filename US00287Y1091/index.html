The weight · Goodwill, debt, dividend

$89.94B in acquired rights sit on the books.

The Allergan deal — and the bolt-ons since — left AbbVie with combined goodwill and intangibles of $89.94B, against just $5.63B in net property, plant, and equipment. The arithmetic puts stockholders' equity below zero. The cash flow doesn't seem to mind.

The takeaway · Fifteen cents

Out of every dollar in, 15¢ makes it out.

$61.16B walks in the door. Cost of revenue, sales, research, the long tail of Allergan's amortization — all of it takes its cut. Eleven and a half billion walks back out as dividends. The shelf is stocked. The bill comes due. Both can be true.
