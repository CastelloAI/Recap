The business· 02

Crude in.
Spread out.

A refiner monetizes the gap between feedstock and finished fuel. Throughput and crack spreads — not oil prices — are the earnings drivers.

Refining ~88% revenue

Gasoline, diesel, jet, asphalt and petrochemicals — sold wholesale and through ~7,400 branded outlets.

Renewable Diesel ~8% revenue

Diamond Green Diesel JV — ~1.2B gal/yr of low-carbon fuel and sustainable aviation fuel.

Ethanol ~4% revenue

Twelve corn-ethanol plants in the U.S. Midwest, producing fuel-grade ethanol and co-products for blenders.
