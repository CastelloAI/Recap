The takeaway · in two numbers
$716.92B walks in.
$7.70B walks out.

Everything between is the bet. Amazon doesn't pay a dividend, doesn't buy back stock, and at the rate it's deploying capex, it doesn't intend to.

The everything store became the everywhere infrastructure. Whatever the next decade rents — compute, fulfillment, satellites, healthcare — it'll rent it from here.
