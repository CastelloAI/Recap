§ I

The Gulf South wakes, and somewhere a turbine spins to keep the lights on. Every kilowatt is rate-regulated — and that is the whole story.

The Business · One pipe, one rate

A pure-play regulated utility, four states wide. Revenue arrives through the meter — set by formula, passed through on fuel.

After winding down the merchant nuclear arm in 2022, Entergy is what's left: generate, transmit, distribute, bill. Retail rates are filed with state regulators. Fuel costs are passed through. The cash flow is calm by design.

The Weight · Capital Structure

A leveraged balance sheet — by design.

Twenty-four billion dollars of debt sits against fourteen billion of equity. Heavy is normal here. Regulated utilities borrow against rate-base certainty; the lenders price it like a bond.
