07The bet · Deleverage

One bench, tipping.

Management has put down the chequebook. The 2021 merger with DuPont’s Nutrition & Biosciences arm brought scale — and $5.99B of debt that now runs the agenda.

Operating cash flowFY25$850M
CapexFY25$594M
Free cash flowFY25$256M

Operating marginFY25−3.5%

The plan is unromantic and on the record: pay down debt, hold the dividend, sell what isn’t core. Pharma Solutions is sold; Soy, Concentrates & Lecithin are next on the block. No buybacks. No new M&A.
