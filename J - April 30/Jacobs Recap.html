Where every dollar goes.

A services business is mostly people. Three quarters of every dollar leaves the door as labor before it ever becomes profit.

Cost of RevenueThe hours billed back as the work itself 75%
SG&AThe desks behind the desks 10%
Amortization of intangiblesThe price of past acquisitions 3%
Restructuring & transactionThe cost of reshaping the firm 2%
What's left becomes operating margin · 7.2¢ on the dollar — the surplus a labor-heavy services firm produces when it works.
