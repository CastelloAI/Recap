02  How it makes money

Two rails. One stack.

Fortinet earns through hardware appliances — FortiGate firewalls, ASIC-driven security processors, wireless points — and through subscription services riding on top of them: FortiGuard threat intel, FortiCare support, cloud-delivered SASE and SecOps.

Rail 01 · Hardware
Products
~36% of revenue one-time

FortiGate appliances, wireless access points, ASIC-based security processors. The boxes that draw the line.

Rail 02 · Subscriptions
Services
~64% of revenue recurring

FortiGuard threat intel, FortiCare support, cloud-delivered SASE and SecOps. The engine of recurring, predictable cash flow.

+  The fastest rail

Unified SASE billings grew +40% YoY in Q4 '25 — the fastest-growing slice of the services rail, and the front line of every quarter ahead.
