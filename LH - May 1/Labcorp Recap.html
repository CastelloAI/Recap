The footprint · 5 of 8

A global network. An American business.

Operations cross 100 countries, but 83 cents of every dollar come from the United States. The international footprint is real — it's mostly the Biopharma trial network — but the cash register is here.

The competition · 7 of 8

Three rivals. Three angles.

A near-twin in routine testing. A drugmaker meeting Labcorp at the Alzheimer's blood test. A research-services giant chipping at the Biopharma flank. Tap any name to read the angle.
