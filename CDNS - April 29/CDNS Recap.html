The business · Three product lines

Software
that designs
silicon.

Cadence sells the tools chip companies use to build chips. Core EDA for digital, custom-analog, and verification. IP blocks — HBM, DDR, PCIe, UCIe — licensed by the design. System Design & Analysis for multiphysics simulation.

The model is software-shaped. Recurring subscriptions, royalties, maintenance. Cost of revenue lands at just 13.6% — every dollar that comes in arrives mostly intact.

The scale · A 13,800-person company

Small,

13,800 employees worldwide. Roughly the size of a single TSMC fab — except they design every TSMC fab's customers' chips.

63% of revenue comes from East Asia — where the world's chips are actually made.

The bet · Capital allocation

$2.5B

Late 2024: Cadence raised $2.5B in senior notes at a 4.44% weighted rate — its first material debt. The proceeds didn't fund a factory. They funded itself.

No factories to fund. No dividend — capital returns happen through buybacks. The 2024 BETA CAE acquisition extended multiphysics simulation into automotive and aerospace — bolt-on, not bet-the-company.
