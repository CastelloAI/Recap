A capital-intensive line throws off cash. The question is always the same: track, locomotives, dividends, buybacks — and what's left.

06 · The bet

$4.36B in. Three doors out.

FY '25 operating cash flow split between the rails, the shareholders, and what's left over. The Sankey is the shape of capital allocation in a Class I railroad.

Two-point-two billion went back into the network — track, locomotives, infrastructure. Another $1.75B went to shareholders in dividends and buybacks. Free cash flow for the year: $2.16B.
