Align Technology made the clear aligner a category. Twenty-eight years later, 295,000 doctors in 100+ markets ship its trays — and the scanner that prescribes them.

You don't sell 22 million smiles by being loud. You sell them one tray at a time, through the dentist who knows the patient's name.

· The bet · $1B buyback

No dividend. A coil instead.

Align returns capital by tightening, not paying out. In April 2025 the board authorized a fresh $1.0B repurchase; in August it bought back $200M on the open market.
