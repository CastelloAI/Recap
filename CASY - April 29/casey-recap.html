B · HOW THE TILL FILLS

Fuel pours in.
Pizza pays out.

Of every dollar of revenue, sixty-three cents come from a fuel pump. But behind the counter, a counterweight: prepared food at ~58% margin, grocery at ~35%, fuel at just ~13%. Inside sales are the engine; fuel is the door.

The other ~5% is wholesale fuel and a tanker fleet they own outright — the pipes between the stores.
