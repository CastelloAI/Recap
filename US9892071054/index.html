02/ The Business

Two segments.
One job.

Hardware that reads things. Software that remembers what was read. Services that keep both running. Revenue blends the three — hardware up front, attach for life.

EVM · Enterprise Visibility & Mobility
The scanners, the handhelds, the eyes on the line.

Mobile computers, barcode scanners, fixed industrial scanning, machine vision. The hardware that reads the world.

AIT · Asset Intelligence & Tracking
The printers, the tags, the trail of what was counted.

Thermal barcode & card printing, RFID, real-time location, supplies, services. The recurring half — the part that compounds.
