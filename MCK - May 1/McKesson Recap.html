Distribution is the dullest verb in healthcare.
Until you measure it: a third of a continent.

→ The takeaway · the middleman's dollar
08The takeaway

$359 billion in.
Four billion out.

The opening fact was the volume — one in three pills. The closing fact is the toll. On a continent's worth of medicine, McKesson keeps roughly a penny per dollar.

Revenue · FY25 $359.05B
− Cost of revenue (96%) $344.69B
− Operating & other $10.05B
Operating profit (~1.2%) ≈ $4.31B

A penny doesn't look like much. A penny on $359 billion does. 192 years in, McKesson is still doing what it did in 1833 — moving medicine, and quietly counting the toll.
