04 THE BILL · ONE DOLLAR

One dollar in. Fifteen cents out.

The cost stack of a diversified industrial: most of every dollar is the steel, copper, labour and logistics behind the part. The rest is discipline.

The Win Strategy framework — Parker's playbook — targets top-quartile margins and cash conversion among diversified industrial peers. The number on the page is the proof.
