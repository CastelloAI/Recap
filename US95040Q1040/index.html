The bet · pure-play senior housing

Out went medical offices.
In went bedrooms.

A simultaneous, two-direction trade: divest the outpatient medical portfolio, reload the proceeds into senior housing — most visibly the $4.6B Amica Senior Lifestyles acquisition — for a combined ~$11B of net investment activity in a single year.

The competition

Two peers, one template.
