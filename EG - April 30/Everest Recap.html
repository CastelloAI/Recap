§ I · The trade
Most insurers sell promises. Everest buys risk — from other insurers, by the treaty.
The book · $17.50B

Two ledgers.
One reads the weather.

Premiums arrive in two streams. The big one — reinsurance — is treaty and facultative cover written for other insurers, globally. The smaller stream is wholesale and specialty insurance written for commercial clients direct.

The capital · $45.4B

A book of capital,
written down to the cent.

Reinsurers don't keep their cash idle. Premiums collected today fund losses paid years from now — the gap, called the float, gets invested. Last year that float threw off a record $2.1B in net investment income.
