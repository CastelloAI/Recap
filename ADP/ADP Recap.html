— Interlude · 01
A payroll company is, mostly, a plumbing company. Money goes in one end. Taxes, benefits, and bank wires fall out the other. The interesting question is what happens in the pipes.
The business · two pipes

Two ways the money
flows in.

ADP runs two segments. Employer Services sells the cloud HR platform — payroll, benefits, talent, compliance — to a million-plus businesses. PEO Services co-employs alongside its clients under TotalSource, doing the HR work for them.

But there is a third pipe nobody draws — money in transit. ADP holds client payroll funds for a few days before remitting them. At today's rates, that idle cash earns about $1.2 billion a year. The pipes earn the revenue. The wait earns a profit.

Where the money goes · $1.00

The dollar, disassembled.

Cost of revenue eats more than half. After SG&A, R&D, and the rest, roughly twenty cents of every dollar reach the bottom line. It is not a high-margin business — it is a dependable one.
