01 The Thesis

A check arrives every month.

Realty Income owns 15,500+ single-tenant buildings and rents them out for years at a time. The tenants pay the taxes, insurance, and upkeep — Realty Income just collects. Then mails the surplus to you. Twelve times a year.

FY '25 · The dividend year 12 of 12 paid
31 straight years of raises. S&P 500 Aristocrat

It is not a startup. It is not a story stock. It is a landlord — at continental scale.

02The Business · Triple-Net

Realty Income owns the building.
The tenant pays everything else.

Realty Income owns the dirt and the walls. Landlord
The tenant pays rent — and everything else. Triple-Net

It is the simplest business in real estate, and one of the most defensible. Long leases. Predictable revenue. $5.75B of it last year, almost all of it contractual rent. Add a sliver of interest income, gains on the occasional disposition, and a brand-new private fund — but the engine is the lease.
