Interlude · 03

Then comes the bet. Sixty-four billion dollars of capex, most of it poured into silicon and concrete.

The Bet · AI Capex

Concrete poured.
Chips bought.

FY25 capex hit a record. The line goes only one way: up. Datacenter shells, GPU racks, fiber, transformers — the physical layer of the AI era is being built on Microsoft's balance sheet.

A $60B repurchase authorization was approved in Sept. 2024. $57.3B of it remains. The fortress balance sheet — $343.5B equity, $30.2B cash, $40.2B debt — funds the build without flinching.

And the pile of intangibles keeps growing: $119.5B in goodwill, much of it the price tag on Activision Blizzard.
