The takeaway · Closing read

One discipline.
Smaller perimeter.
Fewer dollars in.
More cents kept.

Fortive started as a piece of Danaher. Then sold off another piece — Ralliant — to sharpen what was left. The remaining company is asset-light: ~$105M of capex against $978M of free cash flow.

The cash isn't reinvested in factories. It's reinvested in shares.
