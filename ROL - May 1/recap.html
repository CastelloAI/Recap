05The map

Eighty-three cents on the dollar are American.

Rollins is a U.S. business with international ambition. The home market still funds the routes; the rest of the map is where the M&A flag plants next.

Buy the next route. Roll it up.

Rollins doesn't reinvent. It aggregates. Twenty-six bolt-on acquisitions in 2025 alone — small operators absorbed into the parent's training, billing, and route system.
