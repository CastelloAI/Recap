RESET · I

You have never met an ad-buying platform. You have only met its consequences — the shoes that found you, the show that found your couch.

Chapter 02 · A hundred cents in

Trade Desk doesn't sell ads. It sells the buy.

A pure buy-side intermediary. Advertisers pour spend in. Trade Desk takes a percentage and routes the rest — across CTV, video, mobile, display, audio — through the open internet.

Chapter 09 · What survives

The hero opened on eleven milliseconds.
The close lands on what stays.

78.6¢
survive
per dollar of revenue, as gross margin.

Of every dollar that moves through the platform, Trade Desk captures a fee, returns the rest, and keeps a little under eighty cents of what it captures. Most of the rest goes back to shareholders.

A pure intermediary. Zero debt. One billion in cash. Independence as strategy — and a $500M button to keep pressing.
