The business · three nozzles

Three segments.
One trade.

Equipment up front, aftermarket parts and service behind it — Nordson sells the dispenser, then the consumables, then the recalibration.

IPS · Industrial Precision

Adhesives, by the bead.

Hot-melt and cold dispensing for packaging, consumer durables, and agricultural spray — every cardboard box, diaper hem, and sprayer nozzle that runs at line-speed.

MFS · Medical & Fluid

Microliters, into a vein.

Catheters, balloons, fluid management components, and proprietary medical device technology — the precision migrates from the factory floor to the operating room.

ATS · Advanced Technology

Onto silicon, on a board.

Surface treatment, micro-dispense, and test-and-inspection systems for the electronics and semiconductor lines where the dot is measured in microns.

Sell the equipment once. Sell the parts forever.
