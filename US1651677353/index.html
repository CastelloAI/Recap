The merger · Oct 2024

Two companies became one,
and changed the name on the door.

Chesapeake and Southwestern closed their merger in October 2024. Chesapeake took the new name. The combined book opened on FY '25 — the first full year of operations under one ticker.

The ground · Three basins

Pulled from two corners
of the country.

The Appalachian columns — Marcellus and Utica shales running through Pennsylvania, West Virginia, and Ohio — pull fifty-nine percent. The rest comes up out of the Haynesville and Bossier shales in Louisiana, a thicker, hotter rock close to the Gulf Coast LNG terminals.

The mix · 92 / 8

Almost entirely gas.

92% natural gas
8% oil & NGLs

The other eight percent is oil and natural gas liquids, plus a thin sliver of marketing revenue from third-party gas trading.

Scale, in this business, is measured underground.
