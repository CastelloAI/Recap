The bet · PRGP & Beauty Reimagined

A $1.4B profit recovery, hand-built.

The Profit Recovery and Growth Plan targets $1.1–1.4B in annualized operating-profit benefit. $602M deployed in FY25 capex; buybacks paused while debt is worked down. Early signal: Q2 FY26 prints $4.23B in revenue and $0.89 EPS — recovery momentum, not yet a victory lap.

You don't carry twenty mirrors for free. Some of them broke this year — and the company wrote down what wouldn't reflect.

The takeaway · what survives

Twenty mirrors in.
A negative cent out.

$14.33B in prestige sales. $10.6B in gross profit. By the time the counters are paid, the campaigns are run, and the once-bright brands are written down — the operating margin sits at -5.5%. The company's bet is that this is the year the math turns.
