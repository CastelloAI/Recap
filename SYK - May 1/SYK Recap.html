02The business

A surgical tray, set out in two halves.

06The bet · M&A as growth engine

Stryker's strategy is to keep buying.

Bolt-on. Tuck-in. Fifty deals in ten years. The latest closed in January 2025: Inari Medical — a $4.9B move into venous thromboembolism, paid for out of cash, debt, and an investment-grade balance sheet.

The balance sheet, leveraged but rated.
$15.86B debt · $22.42B equity
Cash on hand: $4.01B. Net leverage actively managed toward investment-grade ranges. The headroom is the point — it funds the next deal.
