A bank that lives behind the register.

A hundred cents in · 04

Forty cents go to people who don't pay back.

For every dollar Synchrony spends, where it goes — visualized as one hundred cents. The largest expense is the same line every year: provision for credit losses. The cost of being the lender of choice for the American consumer.

The dollar that survives is the dollar shareholders see. Forty cents leaves first.

A lender's job is to be comfortable with the forty cents that don't come home.

The footprint · 06

A bank with one border.

Some banks chase the world. Synchrony chases the American shopping cart. Ninety-nine percent of the loan book is here.

A loan book that stops at the coast.

When the card you swipe isn't theirs, the competitor you fight isn't another bank. It's a button at checkout.
