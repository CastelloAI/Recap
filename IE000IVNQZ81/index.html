Interlude · 01

You don't notice TE Connectivity. That is the whole point. A part priced in cents, sold by the billion, sitting between every signal that matters.

The business·two segments

It makes money in two currents.

Roughly four out of five dollars come straight from manufacturers — a direct sales force embedded with OEMs that designed the parts in years ago. The rest moves through distribution.

Transportation Solutions
EV · POWERTRAIN · ADAS

Connectors, sensors, and high-voltage components for automotive OEMs. The same part that passes signal through your EV's battery passes signal through everyone else's, too.

Industrial Solutions
FACTORY · ENERGY · AI

Factory automation, energy infrastructure, aerospace, defense, medical devices, and the AI-driven data center connectivity that's currently bending the growth curve.

Interlude · 03 — Capital discipline

A connector business throws off cash. The question is what you do with it — and TE has spent a decade answering with buybacks, bolt-ons, and a steadily growing dividend.

The bet·capital allocation

Cash in. Cash out. Repeat.

$4.14B operating cash flow in FY '25, $3.20B free. The board returned ~$2.2B to shareholders, deployed ~$2.6B into bolt-ons, and cleared the runway for more.
