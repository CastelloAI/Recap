The business · how it makes money

Rent in. Rent out.
That's the whole machine.

UDR is almost a one-revenue company. Stabilized properties — its Same-Store portfolio — supply roughly 95% of net operating income. The rest is supplemental: utility reimbursements, smart-home fees, ancillary services. A dollar walks in; a smaller dollar walks out.

The bill · where revenue goes

Stack the costs
like floors.

Most of UDR's revenue is consumed before the operating line — by depreciation on tens of thousands of homes, by the property staff who keep them lit and leaking-free, and by the interest on the debt that bought them.

Five floors account for three of every four dollars spent — the rest disappears into the slim gap that REITs distribute back to you.
