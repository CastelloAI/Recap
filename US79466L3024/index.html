The takeaway

Software,
without the software,
still works.

The thesis the company opened with in 1999 — that enterprise software belongs in a browser, paid by the seat — is the thesis the balance sheet still writes in 2026. $14.40B in free cash flow. A $50B repurchase. ~150,000 customers who keep clicking renew.

The bet from here is whether Agentforce is the next cloud — or just a feature on the old ones.
