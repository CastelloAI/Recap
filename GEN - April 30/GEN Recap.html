06 · The bet

A company assembled, brand by brand.

Three names, four decades, one thesis: own the consumer's digital perimeter — antivirus, identity, now financial wellness.

Half a billion devices generate one and a fifth billion in cash a year, and the company spends most of it servicing the price of getting here — buying the next brand, paying down the next note, sending the next dividend.

The thesis isn't growth. It's persistence. A subscription that runs in the background of half a billion devices — and a balance sheet that asks, every quarter, whether the perimeter still pays.
