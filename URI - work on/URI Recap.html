Beat 01 · The setup

Construction doesn't pause to buy equipment. It calls a yard, takes the keys, and brings the bill back at the end of the day.

The fleet · 4,800 classes

From a lift to a generator. Rented by the day.

Two segments do the work. General Rentals moves earth and lifts crews; Specialty handles the trickier jobs — trench safety, power, fluids. Equipment rental itself is ~85% of net sales; the rest is used-fleet sales, contractor supplies, and service.
