02 · How the money is made

Five segments,
one barrel.

A barrel of crude enters at one end and leaves as gasoline, diesel, jet fuel, NGLs, plastics — or a renewable feedstock at the Rodeo Complex. Refining and Marketing move the volume; Midstream gathers and ships it; Chemicals takes a half-stake in olefins.

08 · The takeaway

$132B in.
15¢ out. A river that survives by volume, not margin.

PSX runs the largest pipes in U.S. downstream and writes a $2.4B check in 2026 to widen them. The cycle is what it is. The bet is that the next one finds the same volume — and a wider crack.
