The scale · 03
62Krestaurants
in 155+ countries

If every Yum! kitchen lit up at once, you'd see a constellation longer than the McDonald's system. Forty-nine thousand employees run the scaffolding behind it — the franchisees do the rest.

A single Yum! contract spans roughly the same number of restaurants as every Walmart store on Earth — twice over.

— Reset · The takeaway

Sixty-two thousand kitchens send a check upstream. The check does not stay long.

The takeaway · 08
Negative
by design.

Yum! ended FY '25 with −$7.33B in total equity. Not distress — the math of returning more cash than it kept, year after year. The toll booth runs lean on purpose.

Equity · $0
−$7.33B

The opening claim was sixty-two thousand kitchens, one toll booth. The closing one is simpler: a company structured so that almost everything the kitchens send up is, eventually, sent back out.
