FY '25 · annual recap
The business · the spread

A middleman
at the scale of a continent.

Shippers need a truck. Carriers need a load. CHRW sits between them — pricing the lane, booking the move, and pocketing what's left. No trucks. No warehouses. Just lanes.

Two segmentsNAST · Global Forwarding

Two segments do the work: NAST moves North-American truckload, LTL, and intermodal — the bulk of revenue. Global Forwarding handles ocean, air, and customs out of an international office network.
