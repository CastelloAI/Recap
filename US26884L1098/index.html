The thesis Six percent of America's gas rises from one company.

EQT pumps natural gas out of the Appalachian shale — more than anyone else in the country, from a single basin two hundred miles wide.

— interlude The country's gas does not drift in from somewhere obvious. It is pulled out of Pennsylvania, West Virginia, Ohio — and gathered, mile by mile, through pipes EQT also owns.
02· The business

One company runs the whole pipe.

EQT is not just a driller. It owns the wells, the gathering lines that pull the gas in, and — through a Blackstone joint venture — the transmission system that ships it out.
