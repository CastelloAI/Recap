↓ Chapter 01 · The lease

You don't sell signal. You sell square footage three hundred feet up — with an annual escalator clause baked in.

02The lease · 95¢ on the dollar

Rent goes up. By contract.

Site rental is ~95% of net revenue. Carriers sign 5- to 15-year leases with built-in annual escalators — which is why the cash flow chart looks like a staircase, not a sine wave.

↓ Chapter 04 · The pivot

You buy a fiber business for the next decade, then sell it back for $8.5 billion — and use the cash to make the tower company simpler.

06The bet · pure-play towers

$8.5B in.
$7B out the door.

The fiber business goes to Zayo and EQT. Most of the proceeds go straight to debt repayment — the rest to share buybacks. What's left is a tower-only REIT, with a smaller balance sheet to carry.

The REIT structure forces it: 90% of taxable income out the door, every year, by law. Whatever this company makes, you get most of it back.

One dividend out.
