The bet · $800M, withdrawn
They spend it
on themselves.

MTD does not pay a regular dividend. Instead, the cash comes back through the share count — quietly, every quarter. In FY '25 that meant about $800M withdrawn from the float.

−$23.6M

Negative equity, by accident, looks alarming. Here, it isn't. It's the residue of years of buying yourself back faster than you book retained earnings. $955.8M operating cash · $848.7M free cash flow · only $107M of capex to keep the machine running.
