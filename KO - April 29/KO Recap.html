02How a Coke gets to a shelf

A truck full of concentrate leaves Atlanta.
The world does the rest.

Coca-Cola sells syrup, not soda. Independent bottling partners add the water, the sugar, the cans, the cold-chain — and pay KO for the formula.

Stage 01 · Atlanta HQ
Concentrate shipped
High-margin syrup — the formula — leaves KO's plants to bottlers worldwide.
Stage 02 · Bottling partners
Independents do the work
Bottlers add water, packaging, refrigeration, distribution — the capital-heavy parts.
Stage 03 · 200+ countries
A shelf, somewhere
A shopper in São Paulo, Lagos, Osaka picks up a bottle. KO has already booked its margin.
The result
A 61.6% gross margin and a 28.7% operating margin — numbers more typical of software than of soda.
