06  The bet · Capital allocation

They paid out more in dividends than they earned.

In FY 2025, Franklin returned $683.7M to shareholders in dividends — against $524.9M of GAAP net income. That's a payout ratio above 100%. The cash to cover it came from $911.6M of free cash flow, a war chest of $3.10B, and a balance sheet that runs cool.
