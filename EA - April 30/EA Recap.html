Interlude · III · the ledger

Off the screen, in the quiet of the balance sheet, EA paid its shareholders $2.70 billion — and still ended the year with cash to spare.

The dividend$2.70B returned

A loot box,
but the loot
is shareholders'.

EA generated $2.08B of operating cash flow in FY '25 and returned more than that to shareholders. 17.6 million shares were repurchased — a buyback the size of a small studio acquisition. The rest came back as a dividend.
