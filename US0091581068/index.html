APD Air Products Recap · FY '25
NYSE · APD · Allentown, PA

The air,
sold.

Air Products separates the atmosphere into oxygen, nitrogen, argon, hydrogen, helium — and ships it to refineries, hospitals, fabs and steel mills in roughly fifty countries.

— I —

You can't see it, you can't taste it, and yet somebody has to make it. At industrial scale. Continuously. For sixty-five years.

The business · 02

Five gases.
A planet of customers.

On-site pipelines into a refinery. Bulk liquid trucked to a hospital. Cylinders to a welder. Equipment for the customer who wants the column itself.

APD is the world's largest supplier of hydrogen and helium — the two gases on this list that don't come straight out of the air.

The competition · 07

Linde
towers.

By market cap, the field is uneven. APD's most direct rival is more than three times its size; the others trade in adjacent chemistries.

Linde is APD's most direct global competitor — an almost identical portfolio of atmospheric and process gases (oxygen, nitrogen, argon, hydrogen, helium) sold through the same on-site, merchant, and packaged delivery models. The primary benchmark for APD's pricing, contract wins, and hydrogen strategy.
