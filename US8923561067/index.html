TSCO Tractor Supply. Recap · FY '25
NASDAQ · Rural Lifestyle
01The thesis

2,395 stores. One barn.

Eighty-eight years on, the country store still sells feed, fence, and Friday-night shotgun shells — only now there are 2,395 of them across 49 states, plus 207 Petsense outposts, all run from a single warehouse mind in Brentwood, Tennessee.

Founded 1938 · Brentwood, TN Mkt cap · $21.47B
· · ·

Most retailers chase the city. Tractor Supply walked the other way — into the gravel lots and county roads where the wallet is quieter and the loyalty louder.

Forty-nine states, one missing.

08The takeaway

2,395 stores in.
9½¢ back out.

The hero opened with the count of barns. The math closes with what survives the trip — nine and a half cents of operating profit per dollar that walks in. The country store still works. Quietly.
