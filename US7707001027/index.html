— A note before we start —

Twelve years ago, a brokerage account was a phone call and a fee. Now it's a swipe — and the house collects on the order, not the trade.

02How it earns

Three pipes,
one funnel.

Robinhood doesn't charge for trades. It earns on the order flow, the cash you leave behind, and the subscription the most active users pay for the privilege of paying nothing else.

01
Transaction revenue
Payment for order flow + commissions on options, crypto, equities, futures, event contracts.
~48%
02
Net interest income
Margin lending, cash-sweep balances, securities lending, the credit card.
~32%
03
Other — mostly Gold
Robinhood Gold subscriptions, proxy revenues, interchange. Recurring. Sticky.
~20%

In Q4 '25, options and crypto led the transaction line. Net interest carried roughly a third of quarterly revenue — the boring engine behind the loud app.
