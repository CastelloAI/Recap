The business · how rock becomes revenue

A quarry, a crusher,
and a contract.

Vulcan extracts crushed stone, sand, and gravel — and sells it to whoever is paving, pouring, or building. Aggregates drive the company. Asphalt and concrete are the downstream finishers, fed by Vulcan's own quarries.

The takeaway

A century of rock,
paid in pennies.

The hero promised that America is built on crushed stone. The arithmetic adds it up: of every dollar of revenue, thirteen cents survive to the bottom line — and on $7.94B in sales, that thirteen funds the dividend, the buybacks, and the next decade of quarries.
