02 · The business · DC to DC

48 volts in.
0.8 volts out.

An AI server arrives with rack-level voltage. A GPU asks for almost none of it. MPS designs the power-management ICs that bridge that gap — DC-DC converters, power modules, gate drivers, battery management — sold to OEMs and distributors. Their proprietary BCD process integrates the whole power system onto a single chip.

Six end markets buy that bridge: enterprise data, automotive, industrial, storage & computing, communications, consumer. Enterprise data and automotive are pulling hardest.

08 · The takeaway

We opened
with current
flowing in.

Out the other side of the chip, of every dollar of revenue MPS books, about twenty-six cents survive as operating profit and twenty-four cents as free cash. None of it goes to lenders — the company doesn't have any.

One bet, fourteen years long: that the world would keep needing more electrons in smaller spaces. So far, the bet keeps clearing.
