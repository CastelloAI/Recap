The business · 02

Three engines.
One flywheel.

KKR pulls fees from money it manages, earnings from an insurer it owns, and dividends from companies it holds. Each engine feeds the next.

Engine I
Asset management
$4.1Bmgmt fees · '25
Private equity, private credit, real assets, infrastructure. 770+ deals, $790B of transactions in the firm's history. Capital markets adds another $930M in transaction fees.
Engine II
Insurance · Global Atlantic
$219Baum on float
Retirement, life, reinsurance — premiums paid in now, claims paid out later. KKR invests the gap. The float becomes the fuel for the asset manager next door.
Engine III
Strategic holdings
19operating cos.
Long-dated stakes in 19 portfolio companies. The firm doesn't sell — it collects dividends. Together with FRE and Insurance, this engine is part of the ~85% of segment earnings KKR now calls "durable."
