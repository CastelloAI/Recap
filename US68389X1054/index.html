Chapter 05 · The bet

The capex ate the cash flow.

For the first time in living memory, Oracle spent more building data centers than its operations earned. The math is plain: $21.21B in capex against $20.82B in operating cash. The gap shows up where you'd expect — negative free cash flow, and a balance sheet leaning hard into debt to keep up.

Dividends still went out the door — $4.74B, rising to $1.70/share. Buybacks, almost a footnote at $600M. The capital allocation message is direct: build the racks, and pay the holders to wait.
