The bill · 07

You don't buy Seagen
with what's in the till. $65.15B in debt against $1.14B in cash. A balance sheet built on conviction — and on credit.

Total equity is $86.48B. Intangibles, ~$125B. Goodwill, ~$71B. The asset base is mostly the things Pfizer bought to grow — and the bill for buying them sits across the page.

Operating cash flow of $11.70B, free cash flow of $9.07B: enough to feed the dividend, not enough to refill the till. Buybacks are paused while the company de-leverages.
The competition doesn't sit still. One rival is bigger and faster. Another shares a partner. The third makes the drug everyone wants.
The room · who else is on the shelf · 08

Three rivals, three problems.

Bottles sized by market cap · tap any rival below for the relationship.

AstraZeneca
Faster-growing rival
AZN
$230.48B
Why it matters

Direct overlap in oncology, vaccines, and rare cardiovascular — including ADCs, PARP inhibitors, lung cancer, and ATTR cardiomyopathy. Revenue growth of +8.63% YoY pressures Pfizer's franchises from above. P/E TTM 30.47.

Bristol-Myers Squibb
Partner & rival · Eliquis
BMY
$122.85B
Why it matters

Head-to-head in immuno-oncology (Opdivo vs. Pfizer's checkpoint & ADC portfolio) and hematology — but Eliquis is co-promoted by both companies, creating partnership and competitive tension at the same desk. Revenue $48.19B, growth -0.22% YoY, P/E 17.41.

Eli Lilly
The looming threat
LLY
$875.88B
Why it matters

Mounjaro and Zepbound made Lilly the GLP-1 incumbent — and motivated Pfizer's Metsera acquisition. Lilly's oncology and immunology expansion increasingly overlaps Pfizer's core. Revenue $65.18B, growing +44.70% YoY. P/E 42.44. Five-and-a-half times Pfizer's market cap.
