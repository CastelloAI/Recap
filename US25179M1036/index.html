06The bet · capex & Coterra

$3.59B back into the ground. Then a merger.

Devon spent $3.59B on capex in 2025 — most of it concentrated in the Delaware Basin. Then it agreed to do something bigger: combine with Coterra Energy in an all-stock deal expected to close Q2 2026.

The strategic logic is geographic. Both companies sit on overlapping Permian acreage. The merger doesn't diversify Devon — it concentrates it.
