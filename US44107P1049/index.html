01 The thesis

81 hotels.
43,400 keys.
One landlord.

The world's largest publicly-traded lodging REIT doesn't run the hotels — it owns the buildings the Ritz, Westin, Four Seasons, Hyatt, Marriott and Hilton stand inside.

$14.15B mkt cap · $6.1B FY '25 revenue · +7.57% YoY

Most landlords collect rent. Host collects room nights — the rent goes up when the city's in town.

02 How it earns

Three faucets,
one lobby.

Host doesn't operate hotels — it owns the real estate and contracts the Marriotts and Hyatts of the world to run them. Money flows up from three streams: the room, the restaurant, and the everything-else.

In Q4 '25 the basin filled with $1.60B; $0.31 per share survived to earnings.
