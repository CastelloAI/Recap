Microchip makes the embedded brains — microcontrollers, analog, memory — for more than 100,000 customers. From the door lock to the dashboard, MCHP is the part you don't see.

FY 2025 · annual recap

A small business of small parts — sold one chip at a time, into a hundred thousand different products.

The business · $4.40B FY25

Two channels.
One product line that runs everything.

Microchip ships through two channels — direct sellers and global distributors — into more than 100,000 OEMs. A second segment, technology licensing, rents out SuperFlash and other embedded IP. The chip business does the heavy lifting.

The weight · balance sheet

Microsemi cost something.

In 2018, MCHP bought Microsemi. The strategy worked — the bill is still being paid. Goodwill peaked above $13B in 2019; a steady amortization since.

A signature · capital return

Ninety-three quarters,
in a row.

MCHP has paid a dividend every quarter for 93 consecutive periods, and raised it for 22 consecutive years. A streak built through cycles like this one.

Capital return is the spine. A $4.0B buyback runs alongside — about $2.4B already executed.

But MCHP is not alone in the embedded socket. Three rivals fight for the same design wins.
