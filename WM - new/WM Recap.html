The business· 02

Five segments. One pipeline.

Trash leaves the curb and threads a single network — collected, hauled, sorted, buried, and increasingly burned for power. Each step earns a different margin; together they make a $25-billion business.

01 · Collection & disposal — East & West Tiers
The core. Trucks, routes, contracts.
Long-term municipal and commercial contracts with disciplined pricing. The majority of revenue lives here.
02 · Recycling Processing & Sales
Material reborn.
Single-stream and commodity recycling — automation investments are reshaping unit economics.
03 · WM Renewable Energy
Landfill gas → grid.
Methane captured at the dome is processed into renewable natural gas — a sustainability-linked, growing line.
04 · Healthcare Solutions · new
Stericycle, absorbed.
Medical waste and secure information destruction — $7.2 B bought, November 2024.
