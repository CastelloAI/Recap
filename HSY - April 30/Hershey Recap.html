A note before we begin

It started in 1894, in a town named after the man. A hundred and thirty‑two years later, the town is still named after the man — and so is most of the candy aisle.

A note on capital

You don't get to a hundred and thirty‑two years by being clever with money. You get there by being boringly consistent with it.

The bet · Where capital actually goes

A balance sheet
built like a vault.

Closing

One company sells a hundred and ten million dollars of candy a day. Twelve cents of every dollar makes it out. The rest pays for the chocolate.
