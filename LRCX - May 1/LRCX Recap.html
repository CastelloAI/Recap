Every chip in your phone, your car, your laptop, your datacenter began as a polished disc of silicon. Lam Research builds the tools that etch, deposit, and clean it into shape.

The business · Two channels

A tool sold,
and a tool serviced.

One Lam etch tool can run for a decade inside a fab. The first sale is the headline; the spare parts, services, and upgrades that follow are the recurring tide.
