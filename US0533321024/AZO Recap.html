06 The bet

Twenty-seven years of buying itself.

Since 1998, AutoZone has spent $36.3 billion repurchasing its own stock, retiring roughly 89% of the shares that ever existed. The board authorized another $1.5B tranche in October.

08 The takeaway

What survives.

$18.94 billion in sales. Forty-seven cents to cost of revenue, thirty-three to overhead, six to depreciation and interest. Fifteen cents survive to the bottom line — and almost all of those are spent buying back the stock.
