Iron Mountain began in a literal mountain — a depleted iron mine in upstate New York, repurposed in 1951 to hold what corporations could not afford to forget.

The business · two streams

It rents space.
And it rents time.

Storage rental — the box on the shelf, the rack in the cage — is the recurring spine. Service revenue — retrieval, digitization, asset disposition — is the second stream, faster-growing and increasingly digital.
