02How it makes money

Mobile pours daily.
Console arrives in waves.

Mobile — Zynga's Toon Blast, Match Factory, Empires & Puzzles — is now 52% of revenue: a steady drip of micro-purchases, ads, and live ops. Console and PC carry the marquee names — GTA Online, NBA 2K, Red Dead Redemption 2 — heavier per release, slower to refill.

05Footprint

Sixty cents at home.
Forty cents abroad.

Take-Two reports geography in two strokes. The split is steady, the U.S. consumer is dominant — but a meaningful share of every dollar is earned outside it.

U.S. and international share the same pipeline — same titles, same launch windows. When GTA VI ships, the splash hits both pools at once.

08The takeaway
