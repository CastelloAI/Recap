Chapter 02 · The fold

Two brands, one sheet.

In July 2024, Smurfit Kappa (Dublin, est. 1934) and WestRock (Atlanta) folded into a single company — headquartered in Dublin, listed in New York, ticker SW.

The combined company is now headquartered in Dublin, listed on the NYSE, with $22.08B in market cap and a footprint that spans six continents.

Chapter 03 · How it works

From the forest
to the shelf.

Vertically integrated. The company owns each step of the chain — fiber, mill, plant, box — so the margin compounds inside the wall.

Fiber

Sourced from the company's own recycling streams and forestry operations.

Step 01 · own supply

57 paper mills

Pulped, screened, pressed, dried — into containerboard, paperboard, kraft paper.

Step 02 · turn pulp into board

450 converting plants

Board becomes corrugated containers, folding cartons, paper sacks, bag-in-box, graphic board.

Step 03 · cut, score, glue

The box

Sold into food & beverage, e-commerce, retail, consumer goods, industrial, foodservice.

Step 04 · margin captured end-to-end

The result: a 19.4% gross margin and a 5.5% operating margin on $31.18B in sales — thin per box, enormous in aggregate.
