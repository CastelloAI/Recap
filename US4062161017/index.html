03 A hundred cents in

Where every dollar goes.

For each dollar of revenue Halliburton books, eighty-four cents leaves the door immediately — paid out to crews, chemicals, sand, fuel and steel. What survives is what funds everything else.

$1.00 of revenue → 15¢ retained
Of every dollar, ≈15¢ survives the cost of revenue — out of which the company funds capex, dividends, buybacks and the next technology bet.

Eighty cents to keep the lights on. Twenty to decide what the next decade looks like.
