The work
behind the work.

Eighty-five percent of the Fortune 500 don't run on ServiceNow because they like buying software. They run on it because the workflows underneath everything they do — onboarding, incidents, approvals, audits — have to happen, and someone has to route them.

04 The dollar, sliced

Sales pulls the largest piece — twenty-eight cents — because enterprise software is a sport of long sales cycles and longer renewals. Engineering and infrastructure each take twenty-three. After overhead, what's left is the operating line.
