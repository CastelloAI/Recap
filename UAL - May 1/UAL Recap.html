The competition · 07/ Same sky

Three carriers. One sky.

Delta flies bigger. American flies leaner — and weaker. Ryanair circles Europe, picking off the short-haul feeders that fill United's widebodies.

The takeaway · 08/ Landing

Of every dollar that boards,
only a few land.

United started the year with $59 billion in revenue and finished it with $2.56 billion in free cash. The rest paid the people, the fuel, the airframes, and the debt. The next dollar is already in motion — somewhere over the Atlantic, right now.
