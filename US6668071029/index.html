08The takeaway

Five domains.
Ten cents kept.

The thesis at the top was breadth — land, sea, air, space, cyber. The thesis at the bottom is what survives: 10.8 cents of every dollar that came in. The customer is the same. The bet — the wing in Palmdale — is what changes the shape of the next decade.
