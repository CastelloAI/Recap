The business · how a chip earns

Mixed‑signal silicon, sold to the people who make cars.

NXP designs high‑performance mixed‑signal chips and sells them — direct and through distributors — to OEMs and Tier 1 suppliers. Automotive is the heart of the business; industrial, mobile, and comms infrastructure round it out.

The bet · 2025 acquisitions

Three new chips on the board.
Software‑defined vehicle & edge AI.

NXP added three companies in 2025 — each plugging directly into where the next decade of automotive value lives: software, perception, and inside‑the‑car networking.

T
TTTech Auto · software
Software platforms for safety‑critical automotive systems — the OS layer for the software‑defined vehicle.
K
Kinara · edge AI
Discrete edge‑AI processors for inference at the sensor — perception silicon to pair with NXP's MCUs.
A
Aviva Links · SerDes
In‑vehicle networking and SerDes — the wires that carry video, lidar, and sensor data inside the car.
