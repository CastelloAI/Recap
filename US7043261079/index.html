06The hidden engine · client funds

Money sits with Paychex
for a moment on its way to the IRS.

Tax dollars sit in trust between paycheck and remittance. That float earns interest — a quiet, rate-sensitive line that swells or thins with every Fed move.

10The close · dividend-first

$1.45B went out
to shareholders. As dividends.

The company runs payroll for America, then runs payroll for itself. Management has been explicit: dividends, not buybacks, are the primary vehicle for returning excess cash. The board added a fresh $1B repurchase authorization in early 2026 to supplement the ~$160M already executed in FY25.
