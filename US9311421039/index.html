10,900 stores. 19 countries. The flag matters: 82 percent of the revenue still walks through an American door. Mexico, where Walmex meets FEMSA's OXXO, is the largest international beachhead.

Low margins demand high gravity. So $26.6 billion goes back into robots, code, and concrete every year.

The takeaway · what survives

Two hundred eighty
million carts in.
Four cents out.

A trillion-dollar company built on the gap between ninety-six pennies and one hundred.

The bet is that nobody else can keep that gap open at this scale — and that the software, the supply chain, the spark — can widen it before Amazon does.
