The takeaway · A hundred cents back out

Of every dollar earned —
50¢+ goes back to shareholders.

Coterra's contract with the market is simple: 54% reinvested into the rock, 50%+ of free cash returned via the base dividend and buybacks. The rotation lever was the thesis — the discipline of the payout is the resolution.
