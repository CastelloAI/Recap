●  The business · EAF

A recycled car
becomes a galvanized coil.

Scrap is the raw stock. An electric arc the flame. Out the other end: hot-rolled, cold-rolled, coated, painted — value-added flat product at 65%+ of steel revenue.

Q1 2026 came in at $5.20B revenue, $2.78 EPS — the order book is still ringing.

Eighteen billion in. Cost of doing business: nearly all of it.
