The bet · MariTide moment

Ford bet on electric. Then it pulled back.

In 2025 Ford rationalized its EV strategy — cancelling the F-150 Lightning, taking the impairment, accepting that Tesla had already won the price war. The accounting line for that decision sits below.

$17.4B
Pre-tax special items · EV strategy rationalization
