II · The business

Three segments,
one cycle.

A forest pays at three depths. The canopy is harvested. The trunk grows. The roots lease.
01
Wood Products — the canopy

Lumber, OSB, engineered wood. Sold into the housing market. The dominant revenue driver and the cycle's raw nerve.

Dominant segmentCycle-sensitive
02
Timberlands — the trunk

Growing and harvesting trees for log sales — domestic and export — across the U.S., with managed long-term licenses in Canada.

Acreage10.4M acres

Real Estate, Energy & NCS — the roots

Higher-and-better-use land sales, surface and subsurface energy leases, and a growing Natural Climate Solutions business — carbon credits underneath the trees.
