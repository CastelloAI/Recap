The thesis

It senses
what others
can't see.

Teledyne builds the instruments that other companies' work depends on. Cameras that see infrared. Sensors that map the seafloor. Avionics for the aircraft you'll never fly. Founded by Henry Singleton in 1960 — a conglomerate built one acquisition at a time, still compounding.

The bet · capital allocation

A serial
compounder.

Henry Singleton built Teledyne to consume cash by buying companies. Two-thirds of a century later, the discipline is the same. FY25 generated $1.19B in operating cash flow. Most of it went back out the door — into acquisitions and buybacks.

The competition · three rivals

Two its size.
One twice over.

Teledyne sits in a peer pocket worth roughly $30B. ON Semi and STMicroelectronics are within a hair on market cap. L3Harris is the heavyweight — twice the size, head-to-head on defense electronics.
