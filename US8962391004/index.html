Trimble points instruments at the dirt and the road and the rooftop, then turns the readings into software the customers keep paying for. A 47-year-old positioning company, mid-pivot from boxes to subscriptions.

The instruments still sit on tripods. The revenue, increasingly, sits on a server.

02 The work · Three benches

Three instruments,
one office.

Trimble runs three benches under one roof. The drafting bench draws the project, the field bench stakes it on the ground, and the road bench keeps the freight moving. Each one rents its tools by the year.

Segment · AECO
Architects, Engineers,
Construction & Owners

Software for design, engineering and capital planning, sold direct to construction. 70%+ of new annual contract value comes from existing customers, upsold and cross-sold.

Segment · Field Systems
Hardware in the dirt

GNSS receivers, total stations, lasers — the survey and civil-construction kit, sold through dealers. The legacy bench Trimble was built on, still anchoring the brand.

Segment · T&L
Fleets, freight, telemetry

Fleet management and freight optimization — the road-side cousin of the construction work. Where Samsara comes for them.
