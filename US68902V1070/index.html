02 · How it makes money

Two cables.
One does the lifting.

New equipment puts the cab in the building. Service keeps it inspected for the next 10 to 20 years. Service is 62% of revenue — and over 90% of operating profit.

The service portfolio — 2.3 million units under maintenance — is the largest in the industry. Operating margin: 14.8%. Most recent quarter EPS: $0.89 on $3.57B in revenue.

Behind the cab, a counterweight — and behind the counterweight, a balance sheet that has been there since 1853.
