Of every dollar of operating cash flow, ~75¢ funds the dividend, ~37¢ funds capex — total commitments outrun the till. M&A in recent years has run the other way: divestitures of Mountain Prairie and Hormel Health Labs, not large acquisitions.

The takeaway

134 years in,
the question isn't
what Hormel sells.
It's what survives

Six cents of operating margin. Eighty-seven cents of payout. A cabinet of brands older than most public companies. The bet, in 2026 and beyond, is the same one made in 1891 — that the shelf is forever.
