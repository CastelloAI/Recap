Interlude · I

Founded in a 1988 leveraged buyout. Grown through fifty-plus quiet acquisitions. IDEX is what you get when you stack mission-critical components until they add up to a fifteen-billion-dollar machine.

02The business

Three segments. One discipline: precision under pressure.

IDEX sells highly engineered, mission-critical components to OEMs at premium margins — organized into three segments running in parallel.

A · 01
Health & Science HST
Precision fluidics, optical components, micro-precision parts for life sciences, semiconductor, and medical OEMs. The largest and fastest-growing segment — riding AI data center and semiconductor demand.
B · 02
Fluid & Metering FMT
Pumps, valves, and flow meters for chemical, energy, water, and agriculture markets. The original IDEX backbone — the literal pumps that move the industrial world.
C · 03
Fire, Safety & Diversified FSDP
Firefighting pumps, rescue tools — including the Hurst Jaws of Life — and engineered banding devices. Where IDEX's engineering meets the side of a highway.
