The business · three silos

Origin, crush,
and refine.

ADM runs three reportable segments — each a different way of turning a crop into a customer. The first dwarfs the others; the third is where the bet is.

Segment 01 · Largest

Ag Services & Oilseeds

Origination, merchandising, transport, storage, and crush. Soybeans, canola, sunflower — pulled from farm gate to global ship.

Segment 02 · Mid

Carbohydrate Solutions

Corn and wheat milling: sweeteners, starches, ethanol. The plumbing of the American food shelf.

Segment 03 · Growth

Nutrition

Human and animal nutrition — flavors, colors, proteins, specialty feed. Where ADM is buying its way up the value chain.

A hundred cents in · six survive

One dollar, unpacked.

Of every dollar that enters ADM, ninety-four cents leave again as cost of revenue — bushels bought, freight paid, plants run. What remains is the entire enterprise.

The map · where the bushels go

Plowed across four continents.

Revenue concentrates in the heartland, but more than two-fifths of every dollar lands abroad — in South American crush, in European mills, in Asian feed.

Capital allocation is its own kind of harvest — what you keep, what you plant, what you give back. Reset · 03

The bet · discipline over deals

No buybacks.
A dividend.

FY '25 capital allocation tilts hard toward shareholders — $987M paid in dividends, zero shares repurchased. Management is simplifying, not expanding.

The takeaway · revisited
