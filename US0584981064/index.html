§ 04 · A hundred cents in

What the dollar becomes.

Aluminum is the bill. Cost-of-revenue alone — raw metal, energy, manufacturing — eats eight in every ten cents. The remainder is split thin between depreciation, overhead, interest, and tax.

§ 07 · The competition

Three rivals.
Three different threats.

Aluminum's adversaries don't all sell aluminum. One sells fiber. One sells plastic. One sells the metal upstream.

Sells plastic. The most direct rival — flexible and rigid plastic packaging weighed against the can on every primary-packaging contract.

Sells the metal itself. A service center distributing processed aluminum sheet and coil to rival can-makers — an alternative sourcing channel that pressures pricing upstream.
