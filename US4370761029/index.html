67¢ goods on shelf

The opening claim was 2,347 boxes and $164.68B through the registers.
The closing one is simpler. Twelve cents on the dollar survive — and nine billion of them are coming back to shareholders as a dividend that has been raised every year since 2010.

A retailer this large doesn't grow by selling more lumber. It grows by buying the trucks that show up at the jobsite — and waiting for the bill to settle.
