If the floor is pennies, the way out is specialty. So Cencora started writing checks.

06 · The bet · Specialty

One bet, in front.

Cencora is buying its way deeper into specialty — retina, oncology, the high-margin clinics that distribution alone can't replace. The leverage is real: $7.66B of debt against $1.51B of equity.
