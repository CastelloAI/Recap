— The bet · acquisition strategy
The bet· 07 / 09

The conveyor belt of bolt-on acquisitions.

CHD's growth model: spot a fast-moving consumable brand, write a check, plug it into a 180-year-old distribution machine. $5.35B in goodwill and intangibles is the receipt.

Touchland

Hand sanitizer brand acquired to plug into mass-market and prestige channels alike.

HERO

Maker of Mighty Patch — the pimple-patch category leader CHD bought into the skincare aisle.

TheraBreath

Premium oral-rinse brand acquired to compete head-on with Listerine in the mouthwash category.

$2.21B

total debt against $4.00B equity and $409M cash — moderately leveraged, the cost of doing M&A this often.
