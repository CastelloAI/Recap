Beat 02 · The business
The business · two segments

How $52.85B arrives.

Intel reports two segments: Intel Products — chips it sells — and Intel Foundry — chips it makes for others. The first pays the bills. The second is the bet.

~55% of rev

~30% of rev

Remainder · ~15% · Foundry, network, other
Intel Foundry
Contract manufacturing — the same fabs that built every Pentium now sell wafer-time to internal customers and external ones alike. The mountain Intel must climb to justify a decade of capex.

Q1 2026 revenue printed at $13.58B with EPS of $0.29 — the first sign of stabilization under Lip-Bu Tan.

A semiconductor company is its scale. The fabs are huge, the headcount is large, and the bills run in tens of billions.
