07 · The competition

Three rivals.
One capex pool.

All four chase the same buyer: TSMC, Samsung, SK Hynix. Each owns a slice of the process flow. ASML lights the wafer. Applied and Lam etch and deposit. KLA inspects. The leading-edge capex pool is finite — and they all fish from it.
