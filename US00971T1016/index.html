Twenty-seven years ago, a math problem at MIT became a company. It still runs as a math problem — how close can a packet get to you before it has to travel?

The Pivot· FY '25

The CDN business is shrinking.
The replacements are growing fast.

Security $2.24B +10% YoY
Delivery $1.26B −5% YoY
Cloud Compute $708M +12% YoY

Security is now 53% of revenue — bigger than Delivery and Cloud combined. The legacy CDN that built the company is now its smallest declining stream. The shape of Akamai is changing on the way down the income statement.

Built from 325,000 servers — one for every two and a half people in Pittsburgh. The footprint is the moat; the moat is being repurposed.

The Takeaway

The internet still has an edge.
The question for 2026 is
whose edge it is.

A 27‑year-old network is being refactored in flight — legacy delivery shrinking, security and cloud compute growing — while a five‑times‑larger rival pulls accounts in the other direction. $22 of every $100 still survives to operating profit. The bet is that the network was always the asset, and that what runs on it can change.
