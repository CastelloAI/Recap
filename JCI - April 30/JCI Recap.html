● The bet · $5.0B ASR

The biggest
bet of the
year was on
themselves.

With Bosch's $8.3B check in hand, JCI didn't go shopping for the next merger. They opened an accelerated share repurchase — $5.0 billion — and pulled 43.1 million shares off the market in a single delivery. Capex stayed tight at $434M. Dividends paid: $976M. The message was unsubtle.

· The takeaway

The thesis
opened with
what came in.
It closes with
what's coming.

16.6$B
Total backlog · already on the calendar

A water utility's revenue is metered by the gallon. A streamer's revenue is metered by the month. Johnson Controls' revenue is metered by the building — and on the day this recap was written, more than 16.6 billion dollars of work was already in the pipe, signed, scheduled, waiting only to be installed and serviced.

The buildings come back tomorrow.
