Footprint · Where the dollar comes from

A local menu, on every continent that buys lunch.

The company splits its world into three operating buckets. The U.S. is the home market; the international operated markets — mostly Western Europe, Australia, Canada — are the engine; everywhere else is licensed to a partner who knows the country better than HQ ever could.

The bill · $39.97B

McDonald's owes more than it owns. On purpose.

The balance sheet runs a negative shareholders' equity — not because the business is broken, but because the company has spent decades returning capital to shareholders rather than warehousing it. Buybacks shrink the equity line; dividends keep flowing. It's a posture, not a problem.

The competition · Heads up by market cap

A franchise twin, a coffee shadow, a traveler at the door.
