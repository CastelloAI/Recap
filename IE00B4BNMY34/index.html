Ch. 06 · The bet · AI · M&A · CAPEX

AI is the
new billable hour.

The pitch — for a quarter‑century — was reinvention. The product the consultancy now reinvents itself with is generative AI, and the pace shows in the bookings.

$5.9B Gen AI bookings in FY '25, ≈60% of revenue tied to top tech‑ecosystem partners.
$1.5B Spent on M&A across 23 deals in FY '25 alone.
$10.87B Free cash flow after $600M of capex — fuels the buying.
$24.95B Goodwill & intangibles on the balance sheet — the receipts of two decades of buying capability.

A near debt‑free balance sheet ($114M of debt against $31.2B equity) lets the company keep paying — and keep buying.
