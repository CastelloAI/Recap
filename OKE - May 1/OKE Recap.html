The business · 04 streams

Hydrocarbons leave the ground in many forms. ONEOK gets paid on each of them — at the gate, at the splitter, at the dock.

Natural Gas LiquidsLargest segment

Gathering, fractionation, distribution & storage of NGLs — ethane, propane, butane. The biggest contributor to consolidated earnings.

Refined Products & CrudeMagellan, 2023

Natural Gas PipelinesLong-haul transport

Interstate transport and storage of natural gas across the Mid-Continent and Gulf Coast.
