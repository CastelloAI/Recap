II· The shelf · $24.95B

Three segments. One material science.

3M's business is built around three reportable segments. Each draws from the same well of adhesives, films, abrasives, and specialty chemicals — but sells to a different industry.

Cost of revenue runs 60.1% of sales — yielding a 39.9% gross margin and an 18.6% operating margin. Disciplined, but tight. The premium comes from chemistry, not price.

III· Continuous output

In a single quarter, 62 new products.

Q1 2026 alone. The lab never closes. R&D investment and a continuous cadence of new product introductions underpin the long-term organic growth strategy.

IV· A dollar in

Where each dollar goes.

One hundred cents in. Trace each one through the income statement before fifteen survive as operating profit.

The remaining cents — about fifteen of every hundred — are operating profit. The premium for being the company that knows the chemistry.

V· Footprint

Sold on every major continent.

Operating in nearly every major economy. Revenue concentration follows where industry, not population, lives.

A little over half of revenue comes home — Americas first. Asia-Pacific is the second engine. EMEA, the third.

— Interlude Innovation is the surface. Underneath, the ledger.
VI· The weight · $12.6B

A leveraged balance sheet, shaped by settlement.

Years of significant litigation settlements have left their mark on the books. Total debt of $12.60B against equity of just $4.70B — and roughly $5.2B in cash to weather it.

Yet operating cash flow of $2.31B and free cash flow of $1.40B after $910M in capex still funded $1.56B of dividends. The post-Solventum 3M is reshaping itself in real time.

VII· The bet · $7.5B authorized

The capital comes home.

Capital allocation prioritizes dividends, buybacks, and targeted organic investment. M&A plays a secondary role as the company reshapes around its core post the Solventum healthcare spin-off.

— Interlude Diversified means competing in every aisle — and being matched, in every aisle, by someone who only sells that aisle.

IX· The takeaway

A century later, still the small things.

Tens of thousands of products. Three segments. One materials science. The thesis the page opened on remains: 3M is the diversified industrial whose products you have already touched today, on a hundred-and-twenty-fourth lap.
