02The business

Two platforms. One ledger.

UnitedHealthcare writes the policies. Optum delivers the care, manages the pharmacy, and runs the data underneath. Together they turn premiums into $615 billion in annual platform revenue.

05The footprint

A domestic river, almost entirely.

Ninety-eight cents of every revenue dollar is earned inside the United States. The remainder — employer plans abroad — is rounding error.

06The weight

$78 billion in debt.
$133 billion in goodwill.

Three decades of acquisitions — Change Healthcare, Amedisys, dozens before — have built a balance sheet where the past outweighs the cash. Buybacks are paused. The dividend is not.

Goodwill has grown from $71B in 2020 to $107B by 2024 — and beyond $133B today. The company is targeting a 40% debt-to-capital ratio and signals a return to buybacks in H2 2026.

07The bet · $20B in

Where the cash goes next.

From $19.7B in operating cash, the company keeps $16B free. Less than a quarter goes back into the plant. Half walks out the door as dividends. The rest is held against debt — for now.
