Chapter 09·The takeaway
A retailer that sells groceries at cost, and rents the door.

The aisles do volume. The card does profit. Renewals compound. CapEx feeds the next warehouse, the next renewal, the next $1.47 dividend. The model has been running, almost unchanged, since 1983.
