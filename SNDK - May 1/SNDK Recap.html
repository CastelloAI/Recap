The Bet · §05

Two platforms, one node ahead.

Capital allocation now flows in two directions: paying down debt, and buying the next density. BiCS8 is the manufacturing node. HBF — High-Bandwidth Flash — is the AI bet.

01
BiCS8. The next layer count.
Sandisk's eighth-generation 3D NAND architecture — denser cells, lower cost-per-bit, manufactured in the Kioxia JV in Japan. The cost-of-revenue line buys this future every quarter.
02
HBF. Flash for AI memory.
High Bandwidth Flash — Sandisk's bid to put NAND inside the AI inference loop, where bandwidth has historically belonged to DRAM. If it lands, the addressable market reshapes.
03
No dividend. No buyback.
Capital allocation is reinvestment and deleverage — for now. FY25 capex of $204M held tight; the cash that shows up next goes into nodes and balance sheet, not shareholders' pockets.
