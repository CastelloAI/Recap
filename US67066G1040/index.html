02 The business · fabless by design

A chip company that doesn't make chips.

NVIDIA designs the architecture and writes the software. Someone else casts the silicon. Two segments — Compute & Networking, and Graphics — sit on top of a 71% gross margin.

01 · Design
Hopper. Blackwell. The architectures themselves.
Silicon design + the CUDA software ecosystem on top of it.
02 · Cast
Wafers from TSMC and Samsung.
Manufacturing is outsourced. NVIDIA never owns a fab.
03 · Ship
Into every hyperscaler data center on earth.
Data Center alone: ~$193.74B — about 90% of total revenue.
