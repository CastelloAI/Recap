Reset · 04 · Capital allocation
Ch. 05 · The bet · $1.8B

One pipe. Pointed at Ovivo.

$1.05B of capex went into the dispensers and monitors that make the service sticky. On top of that, a $1.8B acquisition of Ovivo deepens the water franchise — and pauses the buybacks.

$2.95B Operating cash flow. Funded the year, the dividend, the buyback, and most of capex with billions to spare.
$1.90B Free cash flow. Comfortably above the dividend ($753.6M) and below the combined return of capital.
$1.8B Ovivo, pending. The bolt-on that triggered the buyback pause. A water-franchise deepening, not a pivot.
0.75× Debt-to-equity. $870.4M of debt against $9.77B of equity. The balance sheet has room to make this kind of move.
