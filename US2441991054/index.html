The bet · capital structure

$43.81B in debt.
Mostly on purpose.

Most of that ledger isn't risk — it's the funding under John Deere Financial, the captive lender that turns a one-time tractor sale into a multi-year lease. The balance sheet looks heavy because the business is heavy.

Capital allocation prioritizes dividend growth and precision-ag bolt-ons — roughly $4.96B of goodwill on the balance sheet — over aggressive repurchases. The bet is technology compounding on top of steel.

The competition isn't loud. It's three quiet machines turning rows on either side of you.
