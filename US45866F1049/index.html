05· Footprint

An American exchange.
With European bones.

ICE began as an Atlanta startup digitizing energy markets. It grew by buying London (ICE Futures Europe), New York (NYSE), and the U.S. mortgage stack (Black Knight). The map still leans home.

$46B of goodwill.
