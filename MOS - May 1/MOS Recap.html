MOS Mosaic Recap · FY '25
The thesis · NYSE: MOS

Two minerals.
Forty countries.
One harvest.

Mosaic digs phosphate and potash out of the ground and ships them to the farms that feed the world. Granular, capital-heavy, commodity-priced — and the only company doing both at scale.

You don't feed the world by accident. You dig it up — and you ship it in two minerals, by the millions of tons.

The business · 3 segments

Three layers,
one vertical column.

Mining → production → distribution. Mosaic owns the column from raw rock to the dealer's pallet — and reports it as three segments, stacked by where in the world they sit.

Phosphates core segment

Rock-mined and refined into concentrated nutrients. The other half of the harvest.
