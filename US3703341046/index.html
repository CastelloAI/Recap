◆   The bet · capital allocation

A 127-year dividend.
And a pet-food pivot.

General Mills has paid an uninterrupted dividend for over 127 years. In FY'25 it returned $1.34B to shareholders in dividends and bought back roughly $1.2B in stock — under a multi-billion 2022 authorization.

Same store, different bills. KHC shrinks alongside GIS. MKC gains share by going small and flavor-forward. TSN comes from the freezer aisle, eating into foodservice.
KHC
Direct competitor across shelf-stable meals, condiments, and snacks — same aisles. Revenue $24.94B, growth −3.5% YoY. Both companies are turnaround stories.
MKC
Overlaps with Betty Crocker and Old El Paso on baking and seasonings. Smaller ($6.84B), but P/E TTM of 8.89 and growth of +5.68% YoY.
TSN
Emerging threat in foodservice and convenient meals. Protein-centric frozen and refrigerated displacing GIS meal solutions. Revenue $54.44B, growth +2.55% YoY.
