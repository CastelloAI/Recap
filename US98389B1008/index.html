09The takeaway

Eight states.
One grid.
And the bill
keeps growing.

Xcel sells permission, not electrons. Every dollar of modernization the regulator approves becomes a dollar of rate base — a dollar of return — and a dollar more on a customer's bill. The current keeps moving. So does the rate base.
