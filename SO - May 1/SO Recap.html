The business · three rivers

Three currents flow into one ledger. One regulated. One contested. One gas.

01 ·

Regulated electric

Alabama Power, Georgia Power, Mississippi Power. Retail and wholesale kilowatt-hours sold under state-commission rate orders — the boring, beautiful business.

Southern Power (competitive)

Southern Co. Gas

Natural-gas distribution to homes and businesses across four states. Different molecule, same regulated rhythm.

4-state gas LDC footprint

Where the dollar goes · cost stack

A bus bar of obligations. Fuel, wages, wear, interest, gas.

Combined, the named lines run about 77¢ of every operating dollar. The remaining 23¢ funds taxes, smaller op-ex, and what's left over for shareholders and the rate base.
