03Where the dollar goes

The bill, poured in.

Nitrogen production is a gas company in disguise. Methane in, ammonia out — the feedstock alone is more than a third of the cost of doing business.

Methane is the column. When gas is cheap, CF is cheap; when it isn't, the column groans.
