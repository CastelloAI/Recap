The business·How it makes money

A key turns.
Rent arrives.

Camden's revenue is almost entirely residential rent — a single reportable segment, no diversification to defend, no business model debate. Pet fees, parking, and resident charges round it out. $1.57B in FY2025 revenue, up 1.92% year-over-year. Q4 alone: $390.79M.
