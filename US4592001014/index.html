§05 · The bet

Sixty-one billion of debt.
Sixty billion of goodwill.
One bet — placed twice.

The balance sheet wears two acquisitions on its face. Red Hat in 2019. HashiCorp in 2025. Together they pulled total assets up by $14.7B — mostly through goodwill and intangibles — and pulled the company decisively toward hybrid cloud.

§07 · The takeaway

One hundred and fifteen years in.
The mainframe still hums —
the balance sheet still aches.

IBM is the rare incumbent that reinvents itself by buying its way forward. Software grows. Mainframes ship. Goodwill swells. Dividends come due. The next chapter is a question of which arrives first — the cash flow, or the bill.
