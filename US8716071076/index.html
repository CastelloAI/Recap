Chapter 01 · The instrument

Modern silicon is too complex for human eyes. Synopsys ships the tools that see for them — and licenses the parts that go inside.

The business · two segments

Two ways to be
inside every chip.

One side sells the software that designs the chip. The other sells the pre-verified blocks that get baked into it. Both bill mostly by subscription. Both are sticky.

Design Automation
EDA + simulation
The software engineers use to design, verify, and prototype every chip — now with Ansys's multiphysics simulation folded in after the July '25 close.
Design IP
licensed silicon blocks
Pre-verified, silicon-proven functional blocks — interface, processor, security, embedded memory — that customers drop into their own chips like solid-state Lego.

Chapter 03 · The bet

Then, in July '25, Synopsys made the largest bet of its life — and put the buyback on the shelf to pay for it.

The bet · Ansys, July 2025

$34.9B

Ansys was bought to push Synopsys past the chip and out into the system around it — multiphysics, structural, fluid, electromagnetic. The cost shows on the balance sheet.
