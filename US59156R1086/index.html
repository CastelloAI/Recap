002 · The business · $57.6B inbound

Premiums in.
Investments in turn.

Customers pay premiums, fees, and other revenues — $57.6 billion of them in FY'25. MetLife holds those dollars in a general account and earns roughly $5.6B per quarter in net investment income while the obligations sit and wait.
