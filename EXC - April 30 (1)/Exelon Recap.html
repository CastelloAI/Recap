Reset · the model
02 · The model

Wires, not
watts.

Six regulated transmission & distribution utilities — ACE, BGE, ComEd, DPL, PECO, Pepco — earn under rates set by state commissions. Revenue is decoupled from commodity price volatility. The wholesale market sets the price of the electron; Exelon is paid for the wire.

06 · The bet · $41.3B

Four years.
One bet in front.

Exelon is mid-execution on a $41.3B four-year capex plan — growing rate base at roughly 7.9% annually. Operating cash flow is real, but capex outruns it; free cash is negative by design.
