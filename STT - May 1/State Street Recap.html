The Custodian · NYSE SINCE 1792

They don't
own the money.

State Street is a custodian bank — the institution that holds the world's institutional money. Pension funds, sovereign wealth, asset managers. $53.8 trillion of someone else's wealth, recorded and reconciled.

Founded in 1792. Older than the dollar bill, the New York Stock Exchange, and every other custodian on the list.
