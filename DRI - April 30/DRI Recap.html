So how does an operator with $1.1B in free cash flow spend it? Three pours from the same pitcher.

The bet · $1.7B deployed

Three pours,
one pitcher.

FY '25 free cash flow: ~$1.1B. Capex was funded first, the rest poured back to shareholders. More went out than came in — leverage covers the gap.

$644.6M

Acquired · 2024

Chuy's Holdings

The bolt-on. Tex-Mex casual joins the portfolio as the eleventh sign — slotting beside Olive Garden and LongHorn in the casual segment, fitting the playbook: buy a known kitchen, plug it into Darden's supply chain, keep the accent.
